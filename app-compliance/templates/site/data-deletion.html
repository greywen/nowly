{{productName}} Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: {{lastUpdated}}

1. Remove the local connection

Open calendar settings in {{productName}}, locate the connected {{providerLabel}} account, and choose Disconnect. Nowly removes the locally stored account connection, OAuth tokens, subscription settings, and associated local event cache.

2. Submit a deletion request

To ask about or request deletion of other information that the product may hold, email {{dataDeletionEmail}}. Include the product name, enough account information to identify the connection, and the scope of your request. Do not send passwords, OAuth tokens, or complete calendar exports.

We will reasonably verify the requester and respond according to the applicable process. If no server-side account or data exists, the response will explain that.

3. Original data in Google

Disconnecting {{productName}} does not delete the original calendars, events, or account information held by {{providerLabel}}. Manage that original data through Google’s account or calendar interfaces.

4. Need help?

If you cannot find the disconnect option, contact {{supportEmail}} or visit the Support page.
